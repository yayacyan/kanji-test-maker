Smoother experience on iPhone

  • Fixed an issue where the "Edit / Preview / Answers" bar at the bottom would float awkwardly over the middle of the screen while typing.
  • Fixed reading-hint text (furigana) overlapping the answer box in Safari.
  • The print button sometimes did nothing inside in-app browsers (such as LINE or Discord). It now shows a message explaining why, with a suggestion to reopen the page in Safari or Chrome.
  • For okurigana (verb-ending) questions, entering the kanji and the okurigana in separate fields is now recognized correctly.
  • The "Create your own" form now shows a short explanation for each question type, so it's clearer what to type into each field.
